A LITTLE BACKGROUND

About me

A Brief Introduction

I’m a thesis-based MSc student in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics at McGill University, supervised by Dr. Ameer Dharamshi.

I also completed my BSc in Statistics & Computer Science at McGill. I’m currently exploring small area estimation and post-selection inference.

Before coming to McGill, I completed the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme at Halifax Grammar School in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

A Little More About Me

What I enjoy most about statistics is finding patterns in data and trying to understand what might explain them. Sometimes those patterns support what we already think; other times, they make us rethink our assumptions. There’s something romantic about that to me: observations can lead to new ideas, and those ideas can be tested against data.

I’m especially interested in how we draw conclusions from data, and how we account for the uncertainty in those conclusions. I like working through challenging problems, whether that means making sense of an abstract concept or testing an idea in code. Being stuck can be frustrating, but I love the feeling when something finally clicks.

Outside of my studies, I enjoy basketball, spending time outdoors, and street dance, especially popping and locking. I also love listening to music, taking photos, and playing chess and board games.
